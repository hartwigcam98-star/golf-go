The Legends Club - two-storey walk-out clubhouse, L-plan: west wing on the frame axes + north wing turned ~14 degrees. 3 main blocks + 1 part (5 blocks)
Main frame:
0 H   west wing, long hip roof ridge E-W (~10 yd)
1 GV  small gabled porch projecting from the south side of the west wing
2 F   east stone patio/terrace at the upper entrance level, above the walk-out ground (deck, 0.5 yd)
Part (da -14, origin 5,-9.75): north wing
  GV  north wing main roof, ridge along the wing (~8 yd)
  H   hipped north pavilion section with its own cross ridge (~9 yd)
  GU  central cross hip / projecting bay across the wing (~9.7 yd)
  T   central arched dormer + louvred cupola (to ~12 yd)
  T   clock tower with pyramid roof at the north-east end (to ~10.7 yd)
Fit: median 0.09 yd, rms 1.15, >1yd 13.5%
Doubts: the north wing's roof has several intersecting hips (diagonal valleys in the lidar) that are simplified into one gable,
one hip pavilion and one cross hip; the centre crossing is ~0.8 yd low in the model. A low strip north of the clock tower
(u 6-15, v -34..-31, ~4 yd) is probably trees/canopy and was left out. Trees west and south ignored. The patio deck height/extent is approximate.
