rossbridge (Renaissance Ross Bridge hotel; golf shop inside) - 31 blocks
The roofs are steep (pitch ~1.2) slate "mansard" perimeters that rise from the eaves to a 23-26 yd crest and then drop to hidden flat roofs (19.8 yd on the main wings, 16.4 yd on the NE wing). Each perimeter side is a shed (SU/SV) with its high side inward, over a flat block.

Fit: median 0.20 yd overall, on the roof itself median abs 0.14 yd but rms 4.5 and ~34-38% >1yd. The large share comes from (a) the steep 1.2-pitch perimeter strips, where a fraction of a yard of misplacement or the many small dormers/turrets give >1 yd errors along every wall, (b) the walkout ground (lidar ground 3-7 yd below the reference, poorly matched by the terrain model), and (c) the separate flat-roofed building/parking deck to the west and trees. Interior of the roofs is clean.
Doubts: many small dormers, chimneys and gables are not modelled; the low-level blocks (8, 28, 29) are guesses at use; the west neighbour (large flat roof, u<-36) is treated as a separate building and left out.
