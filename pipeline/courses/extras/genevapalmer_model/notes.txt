Geneva National clubhouse (shared by the Palmer, Player and Trevino courses), modelled from USGS WI_SEWRPC_2017 lidar (~3 pts/m2).
block 1 H: main two-storey hip roof with a flat top along the ridge (centre roof deck / skylight area).
block 2 F: west flat-roofed wing (parapet edge).
block 3 GV: north entry projection, low gable.
blocks 4-7 GV: four dormers on the north (parking) roof slope.
blocks 8-11 GV: four dormers on the south (course) roof slope.
block 12 F: small flat-roofed vestibule at the north-east corner.
fit: median 0.06 yd, rms 0.41, 2.4% of cells off by >1 yd; residual blobs only at the dormer ends and the entry gable edge.
