The Blender upgrade

Since October 24, 2026 every course uses trees modelled in Blender with new leaf textures, and every clubhouse (porches, terraces, stairs and plantings included) is rebuilt and light-baked in Blender. Each hole's light is baked too, so soft sky shading and real tree and building shadows sit on the ground (51 of 51 courses so far). New courses get the same treatment when they're added.
